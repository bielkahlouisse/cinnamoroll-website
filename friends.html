Cinnamoroll's Friends

Meet the animated characters from Cinnamoroll's world. These are cartoon/GIF artworks—not mascot costume photos.

Animated Cinnamoroll and friends GIF • Tenor source.

Animated Cinnamoroll, Mocha and Espresso • Tenor source.

Mocha

Mocha is one of Cinnamoroll's close friends and is recognizable by her brown coloring and flower accessories.

Cappuccino

Cappuccino is an easygoing friend who loves eating and taking naps.

Chiffon

Chiffon is cheerful and energetic, with distinctive fluffy ears.

Espresso

Espresso is clever and cool, and his curly ears make him easy to recognize.

Milk

Milk is the tiny baby of the group and has an adorable baby-like appearance.

Cinnamoroll

Cinnamoroll is the shy but friendly white puppy and the official mascot of Cafe Cinnamon.
