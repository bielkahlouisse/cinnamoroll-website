Fun Facts

Little details that make Cinnamoroll extra lovable.

01

The name has a reason

His tail curls like a cinnamon roll, which is why he is called Cinnamoroll.

02

Those ears can fly

Cinnamoroll can use his large ears to fly through the air.

03

He has a café home

Cafe Cinnamon is central to his character world and his role as the café's official mascot.

04

He is a gentle character

Sanrio describes him as shy but friendly, giving him a soft and approachable personality.
