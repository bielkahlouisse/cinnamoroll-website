Why Cinnamoroll?

A tiny cloud with a big personality

Cinnamoroll is known for his gentle personality, huge ears, and ability to fly. His character combines a cozy café setting with a dreamy cloud theme.

☁

He Can Fly

His long ears are more than cute—they help him fly through the air by flapping them.

♡

Café Mascot

◌

Sweet & Shy

He is shy but friendly and may even take a nap on a customer's lap.
