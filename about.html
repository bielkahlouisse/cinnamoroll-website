About Cinnamoroll

Get to know the little white puppy behind the famous cinnamon-roll tail.

Character Profile

  • Name: Cinnamoroll
  • Type: Fictional puppy / Sanrio character
  • Debut: 2001
  • Home: Cafe Cinnamon
  • Personality: Shy but friendly
  • Special ability: Flying with his long ears
  • Signature feature: Curled tail resembling a cinnamon roll

His Story

Cinnamoroll is Cafe Cinnamon's official mascot. He is described as a shy but friendly puppy who sometimes naps on customers' laps.

His unusually large ears allow him to fly through the air. His curled tail looks like a cinnamon roll, which inspired his name.

The character was introduced by Sanrio in 2001 and has since become one of the company's well-known characters.

A little timeline

From debut to fan favorite

2001 — Debut

Cinnamoroll was introduced as a Sanrio character and Cafe Cinnamon mascot.

Cloud Café World

His character world centers around Cafe Cinnamon, clouds, friendship, and his ability to fly.

Today — Still Loved

Cinnamoroll continues to appear in Sanrio merchandise, events, collaborations, and fan collections around the world.
